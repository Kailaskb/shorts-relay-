# The High-ROI Life: Shorts playbook

Read this at the start of every Short. Update it after every analytics check. Keep it short.

## Brand (do not drift)
- Premium, minimal, calm, ambitious, trustworthy. Palette: graphite #17171B / #202026, champagne gold #D9B76A, ivory #F4EFE4. No green. Red only for danger or "false". No circles-as-decoration, borders, clutter, crypto looks or cheap gradients.
- Content focus: money, AI, technology, career growth, productivity, higher ROI. Viewer-comment replies are allowed when the topic is relevant or the comment is a good hook.
- Look: faceless-shorts "Inside Out" (flat 2D explainer) with the brand overlay in `kit/` (run `bash kit/apply.sh <project>` after `new_video.sh`). Voice `am_michael` x1.05. Burned captions, one gold word per chunk.
- Format: 9:16, 1080x1920, 35 to 45 s, hook in the first line, last line loops back to the hook or asks a question.
- Title format: `<question or claim> <one emoji> #shorts`. Description ends with `#shorts`. Education = category 27.

## Defaults until we have 5+ Shorts of data
- Length 40 s, hook = question or "someone said...", one fact per line, every claim sourced.

## Rules for using data
- Rank by retention (stayed to watch, average % viewed) before raw views.
- Change one variable per Short (hook type, length, or style) so results can be attributed.
- Do not switch strategy on fewer than 5 posted Shorts.

# The High-ROI Life: channel playbook

Read at the start of every run. The youtube-growth skill keeps "Status" and "What the data says" current.

## Brand (do not drift)
- Premium, minimal, calm, ambitious, trustworthy. Graphite #17171B / #202026, champagne gold #D9B76A, ivory #F4EFE4. No green. Red only for danger or "false". No decorative circles, borders, clutter, crypto looks or gradients.
- Focus: money, AI, technology, career growth, productivity, higher ROI. Viewer-comment replies when a comment is a good hook.
- Shorts: faceless-shorts Inside Out + `kit/apply.sh`; voice `am_michael` x1.05; burned captions with one gold word per chunk; the last line loops back or asks a question.
- Long-form: `kit/longform` (16:9, same palette and kit, separate .srt subtitles, chapters, end screen).
- Titles: `<question or claim> <one emoji>` (+ ` #shorts` on Shorts). Category Education (27).
- Custom thumbnails: unknown (set to yes once the channel is phone-verified; then long-form uploads send `thumbnail`).

## Monetization targets (checked 2026-11-08; re-check monthly and when a date passes)
| Tier | Needs | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Fan funding + Shopping | 500 subs + 3 public uploads in 90 days + 3,000 watch hours (12 months) or 3M Shorts views (90 days) | Super Thanks, memberships, Shopping |

| Ads, applying from 2027-02-01 | 1,000 subs + 8,000 watch hours (365 days) or 20M Shorts views (90 days) | same |

- Shorts-feed views do not count toward watch hours. From 2027-02-01, Shorts revenue sharing also needs 10M Shorts views per 90 days, even for partners.
- Apply the same day any tier is met (YouTube Studio > Earn). Until 2027-02-01 the lower ads bar applies.

## Strategy (two tracks)
- Shorts: daily, 35-50 s by default; reach, subscribers, the fan-funding tier.
- Long-form: weekly, 8-12 min; a deep dive on the pillar whose Shorts retained best; builds watch hours for the ads tier. Shorts in that pillar link to it (Related video).
- Posts: 2-3 a week (a poll on the next topic, an image with one insight, a quiz).
- Instagram: every Short on both accounts, first line varied per account.
- Revenue before YPP (the owner sets up the accounts): affiliate links with a disclosure, a digital product, sponsors once there is an audience.

## Variety (inauthentic-content guard)
Same brand, never the same video: rotate hook types and scene layouts, add one topic-specific visual each time, and give every video one original insight (a calculation, a comparison, an engineer's view). Never post near-duplicates.

## Rules for using data
- Rank Shorts by stayed-to-watch and average % viewed, long-form by CTR x average view duration, before raw views.
- Change one variable per post (hook type, length, pillar or layout) so results can be attributed.
- Under 5 posts: follow the defaults above.
- Breakout = a Short at 5x the channel median views within 48 h: 2 follow-ups in 3 days, a long-form within a week, a poll post.
